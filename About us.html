Our Story

Building With Quality Since 2017

Nyeleti Construction was established in 2017 by Nyeleti Baloyi. The company was founded with the aim of providing quality construction services while building strong and lasting relationships with clients.

The business started with a limited team and has grown through determination, experience, quality workmanship, referrals and positive client relationships.

Nyeleti Construction provides services including renovations, extensions, new builds, small commercial projects, commercial fit-outs and maintenance.
